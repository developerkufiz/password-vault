Password Vault — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 28 August 2026

What the extension stores

Password Vault stores the credentials you choose to save (website URL, username, password, notes, folder names) locally in your browser using chrome.storage.local. This data never leaves your device unless you explicitly enable Cloud Sync.

Cloud Sync (optional, off by default)

If you turn on Cloud Sync, your vault is sent to and stored on our sync server so you can restore it on another device.

  • Email + master password: your vault is encrypted on your device with a key derived from your master password before it is uploaded. The server stores only ciphertext and cannot read your credentials. The master password is never sent to the server.
  • Sign in with Google: we receive your Google email address and a Google-issued token to identify your account. For this mode the encryption key is generated and held by the server, so the server is technically able to decrypt that vault.

What we do not do

We do not sell your data, share it with third parties, use it for advertising, or use it for any purpose other than syncing your vault between your own devices.

Data deletion

Signing out and clearing the extension's storage removes all local data. Deleting your sync account removes your stored vault from the server permanently.
