Password Vault — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2 September 2026

What the extension stores

Password Vault stores the credentials you choose to save — website URL, username, password, notes and folder names — locally in your browser using chrome.storage.local. This data never leaves your device unless you explicitly enable Cloud Sync.

Cloud Sync (optional, off by default)

If you turn on Cloud Sync, your vault is encrypted on your device with a key derived (PBKDF2 → AES-GCM) from a sync passphrase that you choose. The passphrase, and the encryption key, never leave your device.

The resulting ciphertext is stored using chrome.storage.sync, which your browser replicates across the devices signed in to your Chrome profile. We do not operate a sync server, we do not have an account system, and we never receive your vault, your passphrase or your credentials. If you lose the passphrase the synced data cannot be recovered by anyone.

Page access

The extension reads the address of the tab you are on only to find the matching credential and fill its login form. Your browsing history is not recorded, stored or transmitted.

What we do not do

We do not collect, receive, sell or transfer your data to anyone, use it for advertising, or use it to assess creditworthiness. The extension has no analytics and no backend of any kind.

Data deletion

Removing the extension, or using "Disable sync" and clearing the extension's storage, deletes all of its data. Synced ciphertext is removed from chrome.storage.sync when you disable sync or uninstall the extension on your devices.
